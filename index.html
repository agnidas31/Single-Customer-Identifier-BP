1The problem

One purchase, three records, no way to connect them

A single forecourt purchase could create a till record, a loyalty swipe and a card payment, each in a different system. The business could see a basket, a member or a card, but never the customer behind all three.

No ground truth

Clocks disagreed

Timestamps and values drifted between systems; busy sites had near-identical baskets.

2The approach

Block, score, dampen, link

A tolerance-based matching model that says how confident it is in every link it makes.

Refine

Clean all three sources into one common data model, market by market.

Block

Only compare records from the same site, date, country and basket type.

Score

Cosine similarity across time, value and quantity: 0 at the tolerance edge, 1 for exact.

Dampen

Scale each score by its share of all competing candidates.

Link

Keep the best match above 0.7 and chain EPOS, Payment and Loyalty into one record.

Measure

Roll linked transactions up into customer KPIs, reported with coverage and confidence.
